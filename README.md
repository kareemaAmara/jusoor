## 📖 About The Project | عن المشروع
**Jusoor (جسور)** is a comprehensive web platform designed to bridge the gap between recent graduates and the labor market. It connects aspiring professionals with real-world volunteering opportunities, enabling them to build their skills, gain practical experience, and enhance their resumes while helping organizations achieve their goals.
**جسور** هي منصة متكاملة تهدف إلى سد الفجوة بين الخريجين الجدد وسوق العمل. تعمل المنصة على ربط الخريجين بفرص تطوعية حقيقية، مما يتيح لهم بناء مهاراتهم، اكتساب خبرة عملية، وتعزيز سيرهم الذاتية، مع مساعدة المنظمات في تحقيق أهدافها من خلال توفير الكفاءات.
---
## ✨ Features | المميزات الأساسية
The platform provides tailored experiences for three types of users:
يوفر النظام 3 لوحات تحكم منفصلة مخصصة لكل نوع من المستخدمين:
### 🧑‍🎓 1. Volunteers Portal (بوابة المتطوعين)
- **Opportunities Explorer:** Browse, search, and filter available volunteering roles.
- **Dashboard:** Track active applications, upcoming shifts, and total logged hours.
- **Profile Management:** Manage personal info, skills, and CVs.
### 🏢 2. Organizations Portal (بوابة المنظمات)
- **Post Opportunities:** Create and publish new volunteering shifts or roles.
- **Applications Management:** Review, approve, or reject volunteer applications.
- **Attendance & Hours:** Manage volunteer attendance and approve logged hours.
### 🛡️ 3. Admin Panel (لوحة تحكم الإدارة)
- **Platform Analytics:** High-level statistics, user growth, and activity logs.
- **Verification Center:** Dedicated workflow to verify organization documents.
- **User & Org Management:** Full control over registered users and organizations.
- **Dynamic Reports:** Generate platform activity reports.
---
## 🛠️ Built With | التقنيات المستخدمة
This project is built focusing on clean, vanilla web technologies without the overhead of heavy frameworks:
- **HTML5:** Semantic and accessible structure.
- **CSS3 (Vanilla):** Custom styling utilizing Flexbox and Grid for a fully responsive, mobile-first design.
- **JavaScript (Vanilla):** DOM manipulation, modal popups, and frontend interactivity.
- **FontAwesome (v6):** For clean, scalable UI icons.
- **Google Fonts:** Utilizing the 'Inter' typeface for a modern look.
